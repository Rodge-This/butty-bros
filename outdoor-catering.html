Why book Butty Bros

Food that behaves brilliantly outdoors

A plated buffet loses its heat the second it hits a breezy field. Street food doesn't — it's built for exactly this. We cook to order from our mobile setup, so every butty, burger and portion of loaded fries goes out hot, fresh and fast, no matter what the British weather's doing.

We made our name at our shop on Gynn Square doing a handful of things properly: cracking breakfast butties, smash burgers worth the queue, and loaded fries that disappear in seconds. Outdoor catering is simply that same food, that same standard, brought to your event — with all the setup, service and clear-down handled by us.

Good questions

Outdoor catering FAQs

What areas do you cover?

We're based in Blackpool and cover the whole Fylde Coast — Lytham St Annes, Cleveleys, Fleetwood, Poulton-le-Fylde, Thornton and Bispham — plus wider Lancashire. Travelling further for a bigger event is usually no problem; just ask when you enquire.

How many people can you cater for?

Anything from an intimate 20 up to several hundred at a festival or large event. Our setup scales — tell us your headcount and we'll size the service to match.

Do you cook everything on site?

Yes — that's the whole point of booking us. We bring our mobile griddle and cook fresh in front of your guests, so it's served hot rather than sitting in trays. It smells brilliant and pulls a crowd, too.

Can you do vegetarian, vegan and dietary options?

Always. Vegetarian and vegan options come as standard, and we're happy to work around allergies and other dietary needs. Just flag it when you enquire so we can plan properly.

How far ahead should I book?

The earlier the better — weekends and summer dates fill up fast. But it's always worth asking about short notice; we'll tell you straight away if we can fit you in.

What do you need on site?

We're largely self-sufficient and turn up ready to go. We just need a reasonably flat, accessible spot to set up. We'll confirm space, access and any power or water details with you ahead of the day.

How does pricing work?

We quote one all-in price covering food, staff, setup and travel — no hidden extras. The exact figure depends on your headcount, menu and location, which is why we build every quote individually. Full guide pricing will be in our downloadable brochure soon.
