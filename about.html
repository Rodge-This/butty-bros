The story

From Gynn Square to your event

Butty Bros opened its doors at 1 Gynn Square in April 2025, on the site of a long-standing Blackpool sandwich shop just up from the seafront. The idea was never complicated: cook proper breakfast and lunch, use good bread, charge a fair price, and treat everyone who walks in like a regular.

It caught on fast. Word got round about the bacon and sausage butties, the smash burgers started pulling people across town, and before long there were queues out the door and videos doing the rounds online. We're proud to have become one of the most talked-about butty shops on the Fylde Coast in a matter of months.

Outdoor catering was the natural next step. People kept asking if we could bring the food to their events — so we did. Same griddle, same standard, now on wheels and ready to feed a crowd wherever you are.
