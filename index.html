What we cater

If people need feeding, we're there

From a 20-head office breakfast to a field full of festival-goers, the setup scales. Here's where you'll usually find us.

Corporate & workplace

Breakfast drops, staff lunches, site catering, open days and team celebrations. Fed and back to work, no fuss.

Parties & celebrations

Birthdays, anniversaries, christenings, garden dos. Relaxed food that gets people talking and coming back for seconds.

Festivals & markets

High-volume, fast-moving, crowd-pleasing. We keep the queue happy and the food coming from open to close.

Film, TV & crew

Early calls, long days, hungry crews. Reliable hot catering that turns up on time and keeps a set running.

Sports & community

Match days, club events, charity fundraisers and village fairs. Honest food that brings the community together.

Something else?

Wedding, wake, wrap party, product launch — if it needs great food and a crowd fed well, tell us the plan.

How it works

Four steps from enquiry to empty plates

01

Tell us the plan

Send over your date, rough headcount and where you are. Two minutes on the form is all it takes to get started.

02

We build your menu

We'll come back with options and a clear, all-in quote — food, staff, setup and travel. No surprises later.

03

We turn up & cook

On the day we arrive, set up, and cook everything fresh on site. You enjoy your event — we handle the food.

04

We clear down

When the last plate's served we pack up and leave the place as we found it. Job done, everyone fed.
